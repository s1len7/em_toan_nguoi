# Agent Instructions

## Purpose

Maintain `em_toan_nguoi`, a simple, beginner-friendly AI self-study guide for Toan.

## Rules

1. Write all learning materials in clear, simple English.
2. Assume the learner is a non-developer with an engineering bachelor's degree.
3. Keep documents short, practical, and easy to follow.
4. Prefer free tools and link to official websites and documentation.
5. Verify current pricing, free-plan access, and usage limits before updating AI service comparisons.
6. Use small examples and practical exercises instead of lengthy theory.
7. Update `README.md` when adding or completing learning topics.
8. Keep future learning topics as Markdown checkboxes until completed.
9. Do not add unnecessary files, frameworks, or dependencies.
10. Do not claim a topic is completed without evidence of completion.

- Prompt engineering
